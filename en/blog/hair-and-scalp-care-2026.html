Healthy hair starts at the scalp: how to care for your hair in 2026

Hair care has moved from the ends to the scalp. More people are treating the scalp as skin, and the new repairing products fix the hair from the inside instead of just making it shine for a day.

By Hot Cut · 4 October 2026

The scalp is the new skin

The biggest change in hair care right now is the focus on the scalp. In spring 2026, interest in scalp care was the highest it has been in five years, and serums, exfoliation and gentle scalp products have gone from niche to everyday. Healthy hair grows from a healthy scalp.

Repair from the inside

The other big movement is so-called bond repair: treatments that rebuild the bonds inside the hair strand that colouring, heat and sun break down. This used to be reserved for salons, but it is now also available for use at home. With us, colour treatments on medium and long hair include Olaplex, precisely to protect the bonds during colouring.

Six habits that make a difference

  • Wash in lukewarm water. Very hot water dries out both your scalp and your hair.
  • Shampoo on the scalp, conditioner on the lengths. It is the scalp that needs cleaning and the lengths that need care.
  • Heat protection every time. Including when you blow-dry.
  • Brush from the ends upwards. That way you avoid tearing out knots.
  • Trim the ends regularly. Every 6–8 weeks keeps split ends away, even when you are growing your hair.
  • Choose gentle products. We use products free from ammonia, silicones, parabens and sulphates ourselves.
