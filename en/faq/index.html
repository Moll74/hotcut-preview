Booking and cancellations

How do I book an appointment at Hot Cut?

Book online around the clock, or call +45 97 12 60 60. If you want a cut and colour on the same day, book early so we can set aside enough time.

Can I book online outside opening hours?

Yes. Online booking is open around the clock, including evenings and weekends when the salon is closed.

How do I move or cancel my appointment?

Call +45 97 12 60 60 or email info@hotcut.dk as early as possible. That way we can offer the slot to someone else and find you a new time.

Can I get an appointment the same day?

Check available times in the online booking, or call +45 97 12 60 60 and we will help you find the first free slot.

How far in advance should I book?

For a regular cut you can often get a time soon. For larger colour treatments, extensions and updos for weddings or confirmations we recommend booking well ahead, especially in the busy spring and summer season.

Can I book for several people at once?

Yes. Call +45 97 12 60 60 and we will find times that fit together, for example for a parent and child or for a bride and bridesmaids.

Can I get a free consultation before booking?

Yes. You are always welcome to drop in for a no-obligation chat, and extensions always start with a free consultation where you get a fixed price.

Do the prices include VAT?

Yes. All prices include VAT.

Do you charge for advice?

No. Advice is free and part of every treatment.

Cuts and advice

Do I get advice before you start?

Yes, always and free of charge. We start from your face shape, hair type and style, and you are welcome to bring inspiration photos.

How often should I have my hair cut?

Generally every 6 to 8 weeks. If you have long hair and want to keep the length, you can wait up to 12 weeks. A fade often needs touching up a little more often.

Can you cut curly and wavy hair?

Yes. We adapt the cut to your natural fall, whether your hair is straight, wavy or curly, so it looks good both styled and natural.

How do I know whether short or long hair suits me?

It depends on your face shape, your hair texture and how much time you want to spend styling. We help you choose based on your style and routine.

Can I bring a photo of the style I want?

Yes, please do. A photo is a good starting point, and we adapt the look to your hair so it also works at home.

Do you wash and blow-dry with a women's cut?

Yes. A women's cut includes wash and blow-dry, and we show you how to style your hair yourself.

What is the luxury hair treatment?

A nourishing hair treatment with a scalp massage that you can add when you have a cut. It costs DKK 159.

Do you trim fringes separately?

Yes. A fringe costs DKK 89.

Colour, balayage and grey hair

What does balayage cost at Hot Cut?

Balayage costs DKK 1,450, or DKK 1,900 with toning, both including Olaplex.

What is balayage?

A colour technique where the hair is lightened with soft transitions so the colour blends into your own shade. It gives a natural, sun-kissed look and softer regrowth.

What is the difference between balayage and babylights?

Balayage gives soft, natural colour with less visible regrowth. Babylights are very fine light strands for a brighter, more detailed effect. Babylights cost DKK 1,800 (DKK 2,250 with toning).

What is the difference between root colour, full colour and highlights?

Root colour covers only the regrowth at the scalp. Full colour gives an even colour all over. Highlights lighten or darken selected strands to add depth and movement.

What is toning, and do I need it?

Toning adjusts the shade after lightening, so the result is cooler, warmer, more beige or more natural. Whether you need it depends on your hair and the shade you want.

How long does a colour treatment take?

It depends on the treatment and the length, thickness and starting point of your hair. Balayage usually takes longer than a standard colour. Call us if you need an exact time.

How often should I have my hair coloured?

It depends on the technique. Full or root colour usually needs refreshing when regrowth shows, while balayage and soft highlights grow out more gently and can last longer between visits. We advise you on what suits your hair.

Can you cover grey hair?

Yes. Grey hair can be covered with root or full colour, or blended with highlights so the regrowth shows less. We find the right solution together.

Which hair colour do you use?

We colour with idHAIR, a Danish professional colour brand: Hair Paint (permanent colour), Gloss (a demi-permanent toner for tone and shine) and Colour Bomb (a colour conditioner for between visits).

What is Olaplex, and is it included?

Olaplex rebuilds the bonds in hair that colouring, heat and sun break down. It is included in colour on medium-length and long hair, and in balayage and babylights.

Can I have a cut and colour in the same visit?

Yes. Many clients combine a women's cut with colour, balayage or highlights. Book early so we can set aside the time needed.

What are the hair colour trends for 2026?

Warm blondes, copper and soft blends. Sharp stripes and cool ash tones take a step back. Read more in our blog post on hair colours for 2026.

What if I have reacted to hair colour before?

Tell us when you book or call, so the stylist can talk it through with you before the treatment. If you are unsure about an allergy, ask your doctor.

Keratin, perms and hair care

Do you offer keratin treatments, and what do they cost?

Yes. A keratin treatment costs DKK 2,000 on medium-length hair and DKK 2,800 on long hair.

Do you do perms?

Yes. A perm including a cut costs DKK 1,400 on short hair, DKK 1,500 on medium-length and DKK 1,600 on long hair.

How do I best look after coloured hair?

Use shampoo and conditioner for coloured hair, protect it from heat and drying out, and follow your stylist's advice on refreshing the colour. A colour conditioner such as idHAIR Colour Bomb keeps the colour fresh between visits.

What can I do about damaged or dry hair?

Trim the ends regularly, use heat protection and give your hair a treatment. Olaplex No.3 is a treatment that rebuilds the hair, and we are happy to advise on the right care. A hair and scalp analysis can show what your hair needs.

How do I avoid yellow tones in blonde hair?

Use a purple toning shampoo such as Olaplex No.4P Blonde Enhancer, which removes yellow and brassy tones. Toning in the salon can also adjust the shade.

Should I use heat protection?

Yes, whenever you use a hairdryer, straighteners or curling tongs. It protects your hair from drying out and damage.

Extensions

What do extensions cost at Hot Cut?

Extensions start from DKK 3,600. The price depends on the amount and length, and you get a fixed price at a free consultation.

Which extensions do you use?

We use Hairtalk tape extensions made of 100% real Remy hair, in lengths from 25 to 55 cm. The bonds lie flat against the head and no heat is used to fit them.

How long do tape extensions last?

Tape extensions usually need moving up after 6 to 8 weeks as your own hair grows. The extension hair itself can often be reused several times if cared for properly.

Can tape extensions damage my own hair?

Not when they are fitted and maintained correctly. Too much extra hair, badly placed bonds or moving up too late can strain the hair, which is why we assess your hair first.

Do extensions suit fine hair?

Yes, tape extensions can also be used in fine hair because they are ultra-thin. The amount and placement must suit your own hair, which we assess at the consultation.

How do I care for my extensions?

Brush gently, use extension-friendly products, put conditioner and oil on the lengths rather than the bonds, and dry the hair thoroughly around the bonds after washing.

Can I style and colour my extensions?

You can style them with a hairdryer, straighteners and curling tongs if you use heat protection and avoid direct heat on the bonds. Hairtalk advises against permanent colouring and lightening, so we find the right shade before fitting.

Do you have extensions I can take out myself?

Yes. The Hairtalk Hairband is real hair on an invisible elastic band with no glue, tape or clips. It takes under five minutes to put on and you take it off yourself. Clip-on extensions are another option.

Hair and scalp analysis

What is a hair and scalp analysis?

With our analysis equipment we look closely at your scalp and hair strands. A handheld camera with strong magnification shows them on a screen so you can see for yourself. The stylist then explains what we see and gives you concrete advice.

What does a hair analysis cost?

A hair and scalp analysis costs DKK 100. If you buy products for DKK 400, the DKK 100 is deducted, which works out as a 25% discount on the products.

Who benefits from a hair analysis?

Anyone unsure which products and care their hair and scalp need, for example with dry, oily or damaged hair.

Can the analysis be combined with a cut?

Yes. You can have the analysis as part of your next visit.

Is the hair analysis a medical examination?

No. The analysis gives advice on care and products. If you have hair loss, itching or other symptoms that worry you, talk to your doctor.
