Hair and scalp analysis

See your hair
the way we see it

With our specialist analysis equipment we take a really close look at your scalp and hair strands. That way we can recommend treatment and care based on what your hair actually needs, not on guesswork.

  1. AnalysisWe examine your scalp and hair with the specialist equipment.
  2. Walk-throughYour hairdresser explains what we can see and what it means for your hair.
  3. Your planYou get practical advice on treatment and care at home.
The equipment

A handheld camera with powerful magnification and a screen. The image of your scalp and hair strands appears on the screen, so you can look at it together with your hairdresser.

How booking works

A men’s cut, a colour and extensions take very different amounts of time. That is why you choose your treatment before you see available times, so we can set aside exactly the time you need.

  1. 1
    Choose your treatmentSo the booking system knows how much time to set aside.

  3. 3
    Pick an available timeYou only see times with room for the whole treatment.

Booking & prices

Why can’t I see available times before choosing a treatment?

Because treatments take very different amounts of time. A men’s cut, a colour and extensions each need their own space in the calendar. Once you have chosen your treatment, the booking system only shows times with room for the whole treatment.

Why do I need to log in to book?

The first time, you register with your mobile number. The system then remembers what you had last time and how long it took, so next time you can book the same again in a few clicks. If you need something else, just choose another treatment. If you prefer, call us on +45 97 12 60 60.

How do I book an appointment at Hot Cut?

You can book online around the clock through our booking page, or call us on 97 12 60 60. If you want a cut and colour on the same day, book well ahead so we can set aside enough time.

How much is a women's cut in Herning at Hot Cut?

A women's cut incl. wash and blow-dry is DKK 520. A fringe trim is DKK 89, and you can add a luxury hair treatment with scalp massage for DKK 159. See every price in the price list.

How much is a men's cut?

A men's cut is DKK 370. A men's cut “krans” is DKK 310, a clipper cut DKK 200 and a beard trim DKK 150.

Why does a women's cut cost more than a men's cut?

Because a women's cut usually takes longer, involves more detailed cutting technique and more thorough styling. The price includes a wash and blow-dry.

What are your opening hours?

Monday 09:00–15:00, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 08:30–17:00, and Thursday 08:30–19:00. Closed on Saturday and Sunday. Online booking is open 24/7.

Where is Hot Cut in Herning?

Hot Cut is at Søndergade 12, 7400 Herning, right in the town centre. It’s easy to combine your visit with a stroll down the pedestrian street.

Cuts & advice

Do I get advice before you start?

Yes, always, and it’s free. We start from your face shape, hair type and style, and you’re welcome to bring inspiration photos. You can also drop by for a no-obligation chat.

How often should I have my hair cut?

As a rule, we recommend a cut every 6–8 weeks. If you have long hair and want to keep the length, you can wait up to 12 weeks. A fade usually needs touching up a little more often.

Can you cut curly and wavy hair?

Yes. We adapt the cut to your hair’s natural fall, whether it’s straight, wavy or curly, so the style looks great both styled and natural.

Can I get a cut and colour in the same visit?

Yes. Many clients combine a women's cut with colour, balayage or highlights. Book well ahead so we can set aside the time needed.

Do you cut children's hair, and what does it cost?

Yes. A children's cut is DKK 240 (age 0–3) and DKK 290 (age 4–8). A girls' cut for ages 9–12 is DKK 390, a boys' cut for ages 9–12 DKK 310.

Which men's hairstyle suits me?

It depends on your face shape and hair type. A fade or crew cut adds definition to a round face, while a classic side parting often suits a square face. We’ll advise you for free in the chair.

Colour & balayage

How much is balayage in Herning at Hot Cut?

Balayage is DKK 1,450, or DKK 1,900 with a toner, both incl. Olaplex. Our final recommendation depends on your hair’s starting point, length and the shade you want.

What’s the difference between balayage and babylights?

Balayage gives a soft, natural play of colour with less noticeable regrowth. Babylights are very fine, light strands that give a more detailed, brighter effect. Babylights are DKK 1,800.

How long does balayage take?

Balayage usually takes longer than a standard colour treatment, because the technique calls for precision. The time depends on your hair’s length, thickness and starting point.

Which products do you use?

We use carefully selected products free from ammonia, silicone, parabens and sulphates. Colour treatments on medium-length and long hair include Olaplex.

Extensions

How much are extensions at Hot Cut?

Extensions start from DKK 3,600. We use Hairtalk tape extensions made from 100% genuine Remy hair, in lengths from 25 to 55 cm. You’ll get an exact price at a free consultation.

How long do tape extensions last?

Tape extensions usually need moving up after 6–8 weeks, because your own hair grows. The extension hair itself can often be reused several times if it’s cared for properly.

Can tape extensions damage my own hair?

Not when they’re applied correctly and looked after. Too much extra hair, badly placed bonds or moving them up too late can strain the hair, which is why we assess your hair before the treatment.

Are extensions suitable for fine hair?

Yes, tape extensions work in fine hair too, because they’re ultra-thin. The amount of hair and the placement of the bonds have to suit your own hair, and we’ll assess that together.

Updos & beauty

Do you do updos for weddings and confirmations?

Yes. A bridal updo is DKK 1,600, a confirmation updo DKK 750 and an updo DKK 500–750. We recommend a trial updo (DKK 500) so you can feel confident on the day.

Do you offer makeup, lash lift and brow lamination?

Yes. Makeup is DKK 300 or 600, brow lamination DKK 450–500, and brows and lashes can be tinted and shaped from DKK 100. We also offer lash lifts. Call for a price.

Hair and scalp analysis

What is a hair and scalp analysis?

With our specialist analysis equipment we take a really close look at your scalp and hair strands. Afterwards, your hairdresser explains what we can see, and you get practical advice on treatment and care at home.

How much is a hair analysis?
