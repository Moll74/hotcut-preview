What are tape extensions?

Tape extensions are wefts of real hair attached to your own hair with thin tape bonds. The bonds lie flat against the head and are placed so they blend naturally into your style. No heat is used to fit them.

We use Hairtalk extensions made from real Remy hair, in lengths from 25 to 55 cm. They can add volume, extra length or both. How much hair you need depends on your own hair and what you want.

Extensions that suit your hair

A natural result starts with the right fit. At a free consultation we talk about how much volume or length you want and look at the thickness, length and condition of your hair. Then we find the right colour and amount, and you get a fixed price.

Tape extensions can also be an option if you have fine hair. Here it is especially important that the placement of the bonds and the amount of extra hair suit your own hair. We assess that together before they go in.

Moving up and maintenance

As your own hair grows, the tape bonds move down with it. That is why extensions usually need moving up after 6 to 8 weeks. We carefully remove them, re-tape the bonds and place them close to the scalp again. The same extension hair can often be reused several times if it is in good condition and cared for properly.

Have your hair moved up in time. Bonds that sit too far from the scalp can become visible and put strain on your own hair.

Caring for your extensions

  • Brush gently and use products suitable for extensions.
  • Apply conditioner and hair oil to the lengths, not the tape bonds, where they can weaken the hold.
  • Dry the hair thoroughly around the bonds after washing.
  • You can use a hairdryer, straighteners and curling tongs. Use heat protection and avoid direct heat on the bonds.

Questions and answers

Good to know

Will tape extensions suit my hair?

They can add both volume and length, also in fine hair. The amount and placement must suit your own hair, so we look at it at a consultation.

How long do tape extensions last?

The bonds usually need moving up after 6 to 8 weeks as your hair grows. The extension hair itself can often be reused several times if it is in good condition.

Can tape extensions damage my own hair?

Not when they are fitted and maintained correctly. Too much extra hair, badly placed bonds or moving up too late can strain the hair, which is why we assess your hair first.

Can I style my extensions with heat?

Yes, Hairtalk real-hair extensions can be styled with a hairdryer, straighteners and curling tongs. Use heat protection and avoid direct heat on the bonds.

How do I care for tape extensions?

Brush gently and use suitable products. Conditioner and oil in the lengths, not on the bonds. Dry the hair thoroughly around the bonds after washing.

Can my extensions be coloured?

We recommend finding the right shade before fitting. Hairtalk advises against permanent colouring and lightening of its tape extensions.
