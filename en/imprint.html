Prices and booking

All prices are in Danish kroner (DKK) including VAT. If you need to cancel or move an appointment, please do so in the booking system or call us as early as possible.

If you are unhappy with a treatment or a purchase, please contact us first and we will find a solution. If we cannot agree, you can complain to Nævnenes Hus (the Danish Board of Appeal), Toldboden 2, 8800 Viborg, Denmark.
