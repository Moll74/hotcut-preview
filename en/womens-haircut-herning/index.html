Women's haircuts in Herning: your local hairdresser

Why choose us for your haircut?

A good hairdresser does more than make your hair shorter. It is about finding someone who understands your hair, your style and how much time you want to spend in front of the mirror. We work with every hair type and length, and we take the time to find the cut that suits you.

Our stylists keep up with trends and techniques, but a haircut also has to work after you have washed it yourself. That is why you always leave with advice on care and styling.

How a women’s cut works with us

  • Consultation: we talk about what you want and look at your hair’s natural fall, texture and thickness. Feel free to bring inspiration photos.
  • Wash: a thorough wash and, if you like, a luxury hair treatment with scalp massage for DKK 159.
  • Cut: we cut to your natural fall, so the style also works when it dries naturally.
  • Blow-dry and styling: we finish the blow-dry and show you how to get the same result at home.

A style that suits your hair

Straight, wavy and curly hair each need their own technique. We adapt the cut so it works with your hair rather than against it. Whether you dream of soft waves, a sleek bob or light layers, we find the solution that brings out your best features.

Not sure whether to go short or long? We look at your face shape, your hair texture and your daily routine. To see what is popular right now, read our guide to women’s haircuts for autumn 2026.

Combine cut and colour

Many of our clients combine a haircut with balayage, babylights or highlights. It is a great way to refresh your look. Book early so we can set aside enough time for both. Colour on medium-length and long hair includes Olaplex.

How often should you have your hair cut?We recommend a cut every 6 to 8 weeks to keep the shape and healthy ends. If you want to keep the length on long hair, you can wait up to 12 weeks.

Your local hairdresser in Herning

Hot Cut is at Søndergade 12 in the centre of Herning, so it is easy to combine your visit with a walk down the high street or a coffee in town. Many of our clients have been coming to us for years, because they know we remember them and their hair from visit to visit.

Besides women’s cuts we offer colour, extensions, keratin, perms and updos for weddings and confirmations. See every treatment on the price list.

Questions and answers

Good to know

How often should I have my hair cut?

It depends, but generally we recommend a cut every 6 to 8 weeks. If you have long hair and want to keep the length, you can wait up to 12 weeks.

What does a women's cut cost at Hot Cut?

A women's cut including wash and blow-dry costs DKK 520. A fringe trim costs DKK 89, and a luxury treatment with scalp massage can be added for DKK 159. See all prices on the price list.

Can I get advice before my haircut?

Yes. You are always welcome to drop in for a no-obligation chat, and you always get advice in the chair before we start.

Can I have a cut and colour in the same visit?

Yes. Many clients combine a cut with colour, balayage or highlights. Book early so we can set aside the time needed.

How do I know which style suits me?

We advise you based on your face shape, hair type and style. If you have an idea, bring some inspiration photos.

Can I get a cut that works with my natural texture?

Yes. We adapt the cut to your hair, whether it is straight, wavy or curly, so it looks good both styled and natural.

Why does a women's cut cost more than a men's cut?

A women's cut usually takes longer, needs more detailed cutting technique and more thorough styling. The price includes wash and blow-dry.
