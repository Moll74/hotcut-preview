What is balayage?

Balayage is a colour technique where the hair is lightened with soft transitions, giving a natural, sun-kissed result. The colour blends gradually into your own shade, so you avoid a hard regrowth line. That is why balayage is so popular if you want a light, luxurious look without colouring all your hair from the roots.

Is balayage right for you?

Balayage is often a good choice if you:

  • want natural, soft colour
  • want lighter lengths without harsh regrowth
  • want a more luxurious colour than classic highlights
  • want a result built on your own hair colour
  • would like to combine colour with a haircut or styling

Questions and answers

Good to know

What is the difference between balayage and babylights?

Balayage gives softer, more natural colour with less visible regrowth. Babylights are very fine, light strands that give a more detailed, brighter effect.

Is toning necessary with balayage?

It depends on your hair and the shade you want. Toning adjusts the tone so the result is cooler, warmer, more beige or more natural.

How long does balayage take?

It depends on the length, thickness and starting point of your hair. Balayage usually takes longer than a standard colour, because the technique needs precision.

How do I maintain my balayage?

Use good care products, protect your hair from drying out and follow your stylist’s advice. How often to refresh depends on your hair and the look you want.

Is Olaplex included in the price?

Yes. Balayage and babylights include Olaplex, which protects the hair during lightening.

Should I book a consultation first?

If you are unsure about the treatment, colour or price, contact the salon first. We will advise you on the best option.
