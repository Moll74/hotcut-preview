Questions and answers

Good to know

Why does a women's cut cost more than a men's cut?

A women's cut usually takes longer, needs more detailed technique and more thorough styling. The price includes wash and blow-dry.

Is Olaplex included?

Yes, in colour on medium-length and long hair, and in balayage and babylights.

What do extensions cost?

From DKK 3,600. The price depends on the amount and length, and you get a fixed price at a free consultation.

What does a hair analysis cost?

A hair and scalp analysis costs DKK 100. The amount is deducted when you buy products for DKK 400.
