Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

If you have questions about how we handle your data, you are always welcome to contact us.

2. What we process and why

When you book an appointment

You book online through our booking system at hotcut.bestilling.nu or by phone. We process your name, phone number, email, the treatment you chose, the time and any comments. We use this to carry out your appointment and send a confirmation and reminder.

Legal basis: contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Client records and treatment notes

So we can give you the same result next time, we note things like your colour formula, cut length and products. If you tell us about allergies, sensitivities or skin and scalp conditions, we only record this if you agree, because it is health data.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the treatment you expect (Art. 6(1)(f)) and, for health data, your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).

Hair and scalp analysis

During the analysis we take magnified images of your hair and scalp with our analysis equipment. We use them to show you the result and recommend the right care. They are only saved to your client record if you want us to.

Legal basis: contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and your consent to store the images (Art. 6(1)(a)).

When you contact us

If you email us, message us on Facebook or Instagram, or call, we process the information you give us so we can reply.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Purchases and payment

When you pay for a treatment or buy products, we record the purchase in our till system and accounts. We do not store your card details; the payment terminal provider handles them.

Legal basis: legal obligation under the Danish Bookkeeping Act (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Photos of clients

We like to show our work on the website and social media. We only use photos in which you can be recognised if you have given your consent. If you are under 15, a parent must give consent. You can withdraw consent at any time, and we will remove the photo.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

When you use the website

  • The AI assistant runs directly in your browser. Your questions are not sent to us and are not stored. If you use the microphone, your browser’s built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text. In Google Chrome, for example, this is done by Google. The microphone is only active when you press it.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type and time are logged by our web host to deliver the site and protect it against abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Fonts, scripts and maps are hosted on our own server. Nothing is loaded from Google or other third parties when the page opens. The parking map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is not loaded from outside either.
  • Cookies and local storage: see our cookie policy.

3. Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We only share it with suppliers who help us run the salon and process it on our behalf under a written data processing agreement:

  • Our online booking provider (bestilling.nu)
  • Our till system and payment terminal
  • Web host and email provider
  • Accountant and bookkeeper

If you message us via Facebook or Instagram, Meta is an independent controller for the data Meta itself processes. We only disclose data to authorities when the law requires it.

4. Transfers outside the EU

Some suppliers may process data in the USA, for example Google if you use speech recognition in Chrome, and Meta if you message us via Facebook or Instagram. This only happens on a lawful transfer basis, typically the European Commission’s decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Bookings, client records and treatment notes: for as long as you are a client. If you have not visited for 3 years, we delete them.
  • Health data and analysis images: until you withdraw consent, and no later than when your client record is deleted.
  • Enquiries: deleted when the matter is closed, and after 12 months at the latest.
  • Accounting records: 5 years from the end of the financial year, as required by the Bookkeeping Act.
  • Photos of clients: until you withdraw consent.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you
  • rectification of incorrect data
  • erasure of data we no longer have a reason to keep
  • restriction of processing
  • data portability, receiving your data in a common format
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

To use your rights, email info@hotcut.dk or ask us in the salon. We reply as quickly as possible and within one month at the latest.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please talk to us first. You can always complain to:

Datatilsynet (the Danish Data Protection Agency), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark · phone +45 33 19 32 00 · dt@datatilsynet.dk · datatilsynet.dk

8. Changes

We update this policy when we change how we process data. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
